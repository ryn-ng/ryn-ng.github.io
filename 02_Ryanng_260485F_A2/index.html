VOLLEYBALL

volleyball Origin and History

  1. Invented in 1895 by William G. Morgan
  2. It Was Originally Called Mintonette and eventually renamed to volleyball due to the nature of play

Rules And Gameplay

  1. there are 2 teams of 6 players
  2. there is a maximum of 3 touches before returning the ball over the net
  3. to score the ball must land in the opponents field/court
  4. the net height is based on age and gender
  5. there is 3 players at the front court and 3 at the back court the back court players only can jump before a 3 meter line from the net to the court to attack while the libero(role explained later) are not allowed to jump and attack the ball at all

recieving facts

  1. to recieve a spike you need a reaction time of 180ms-250ms
  2. the better the recieve the better your attack will become

how to form a recieving platform

  1. place both hands face up and make sure the fingers are touching one another
  2. form a 45 degree angle with both hands
  3. make both ur thumbs touch each other
  4. straighten ur arm and recieve the ball at about where you would wear a watch
